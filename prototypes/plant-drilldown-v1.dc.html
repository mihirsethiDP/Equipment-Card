Drill-down flow

Plant → unit process → unit → equipment

The same card shape at every level. Issues, tasks and maintenance are owned at the level they belong to and roll up: a process card shows what is filed against the process itself, then everything from its units and their equipment, each tagged with where it lives. Click a tile to descend, a crumb to come back up. Both frames navigate independently.
